About Me

Wangyipu Li is a PhD student in the Department of Forest and Wildlife Ecology at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. His research interests cover vegetation dynamic monitoring, estimation of water-carbon fluxes, and issues related to Earth system science. He earned a M.Eng. in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing at Peking University, and a B.S. in Applied Meteorology at China Agricultural University.
