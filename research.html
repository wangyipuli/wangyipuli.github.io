Evapotranspiration: from site to satellite

Eddy Covariance Data Post-processing

I built a framework centred on machine learning algorithms that gap-fills and prolongs ground-based observation records, and used it to produce a benchmark dataset of evapotranspiration observations covering 2000–2020 (Li et al., JoH, 2024; Li et al., ESSD, 2025).

Global Hourly ET Estimation

I build deep learning models trained under a pre-training and fine-tuning strategy, and apply them to very large volumes of multi-source data to produce global evapotranspiration mapping at 0.05° and hourly resolution.
